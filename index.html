    Quality & Certification

    Know exactly what you are selling.

    Laboratory-grown diamonds, explained.

    A laboratory-grown diamond is diamond grown in a controlled process (HPHT or CVD). It has the same chemical, physical and optical properties as a mined diamond. Origin and quality are separate questions: each stone is graded on its own cut, colour, clarity and carat weight.

    How laboratory-grown diamonds are made

    Certificates and hallmarks.

    Every piece comes with the documents that support what you tell your customer: an IGI certificate for the laboratory-grown diamond and a BIS hallmark with HUID on gold.
