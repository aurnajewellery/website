Why AURNA

A stronger offering. A more flexible way to grow.

  • A more inventory-conscious approach

    Offer a wider range without holding it all in stock.

    Precious-metal inventory can tie up significant working capital, particularly when metal prices are high. AURNA helps retail partners keep their inventory lean through ready-to-ship designs, made-to-order options and small, flexible orders, so you can offer more to your customers without carrying it all in stock.

  • Stay distinctive in a changing market

    Compete through individuality, not just scale.

    As organised jewellery brands expand into more cities, independent retailers need clear reasons for customers to choose them. Distinctive designs, personalised options and the ability to respond to individual customer requests help create that difference.

  • Specified and documented

    Know exactly what you are selling.

    Every AURNA design comes with clear specifications: stone shape and carat, metal and purity. Solitaires are supplied with an IGI or SGL certificate, whichever you prefer, and gold jewellery carries the BIS hallmark. That makes it easier for your team to present each piece and answer customer questions.

Quality and documentation

Every piece, specified and checked.

Each AURNA piece is supplied with its specifications and supporting documents, so you and your customers know exactly what was bought.

  • Stone certification: every solitaire comes with an IGI or SGL certificate, whichever you prefer.

  • Gold hallmarking: BIS hallmark with HUID on gold jewellery.

  • Specifications: stone shape and carat, metal and purity, recorded for each design.

  • Quality checks: at every step, from stone selection and setting to finishing and dispatch.

How it works

From first conversation to supply.

We respond to every trade enquiry within 24 hours on working days.

  1. 1Tell us what you need.Share a few details about your business and what you're looking for, through the form below or on WhatsApp. One piece or a full range, every enquiry is welcome.
  2. 2Talk it through.The AURNA team gets in touch to understand your store and your customers, and to go through designs, metals, stone options, quantities and timelines with you.
  3. 3Confirm the details.We share the exact specifications for each piece: stone shape and carat, metal and purity, and certificate choice. Nothing goes into production until you've confirmed them.
  4. 4Made and checked.Your pieces are made or prepared from ready stock, with quality checks at every step, from stone setting to final finish.
  5. 5Delivered with documents.Each piece arrives with its specifications, an IGI or SGL certificate for solitaires and the BIS hallmark on gold, ready for your counter or your customer.

FAQs

Questions retailers often ask.

Who can become an AURNA trade partner?

AURNA works with jewellery retailers, chains, boutiques, designers, online jewellery retailers and businesses building their own jewellery brand.

Do you have minimum order quantities?

We welcome very small orders. Helping retailers start small, test new designs or order for a single customer is exactly the kind of support AURNA is here to provide.

Can I sell AURNA jewellery under my own brand?

Yes. Through white-label supply, AURNA-made jewellery can be presented under your brand identity, on terms agreed with you.

Can you make a design to my brief?

Yes, through custom development. Share your brief, sketch or reference images and we'll review feasibility, specifications and timelines with you. Not every design can be made exactly as requested, and we'll tell you what's possible.

Are your stones certified?

Yes. Every solitaire is supplied with an IGI or SGL certificate, as per your requirement.

Is your gold jewellery hallmarked?

Yes. Gold jewellery is BIS hallmarked, with HUID, in 9K, 14K and 18K.

How long does production take?

Timelines depend on the design, quantity and whether pieces are ready stock or made-to-order. We'll confirm timelines for your order before you commit.

Can I order for my customers without holding stock?

Yes. AURNA is built to support a lean-inventory way of working. You can offer AURNA designs to your customers and order pieces as they're needed, made to order or from ready stock, with small orders welcome. That way you can show a wider range without tying up capital in stock, and our team will help you plan which designs to offer and how ordering works for your store.

Can I see samples before ordering?

Samples or detailed specifications can be arranged for selected designs. We'll discuss this during your enquiry.

How do I get the trade catalogue?

Request it using the catalogue form. We review each request and share the catalogue by WhatsApp or email.

Which areas do you supply?

We supply jewellery businesses across India and overseas.

What are your payment terms?

Payment terms are agreed with each partner as part of the commercial discussion.

Indian numbers without a code; add + and the country code for other countries.
