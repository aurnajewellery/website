Customise

Custom design and development

Develop a piece to a brief: jewellery type, stone shape and size, setting, metal and purity. Retailers can develop designs for their store or their own brand. Individual buyers can ask about a personal piece.

For retailers Buying for yourself?

What can be customised

Four things to decide, with our help.

  • Jewellery type

    Rings, earrings, pendants, necklaces and bracelets.

  • Stone shape and size

    Round, oval, emerald, pear, princess, cushion and other laboratory-grown diamond shapes, in the size you need.

  • Setting

    The setting style that suits the design and the stone.

  • Metal and purity

    9K, 14K or 18K gold in yellow, white or rose; platinum; silver.
