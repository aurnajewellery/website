## 1. Writing rules

**Brand line:** *Brilliance, beautifully considered.*

**Voice:** precise, reliable, partner-focused, premium without being ornate (Blueprint 4.2). It should sound like a reliable, exacting supplier with taste (design.md 2.1).

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| State specifications, process and terms plainly | Use stacked adjectives or luxury clichés |
| Talk about the retailer's business and customers | Use gifting or sentiment language: "moments", "meaning", "your story", "close to the heart" |
| Promise only what operations can deliver | Claim ethical, sustainable, conflict-free, eco-friendly, guaranteed, best, cheapest, investment or resale value |
| Write "laboratory-grown diamond" in specs, labels, cards, the proof strip, forms and titles | Write "lab-grown", "LGD", "lab diamond", "cultured", "synthetic", "artificial", or a bare "diamond" that implies natural origin |
| Use "stone" for shape and size fields ("Stone shape") | Write "Diamond shape" or "Diamond size" |
| Keep "considered" for the brand line only | Repeat "considered" in headings or body copy |
| Use sentence case for headings, buttons and links; page, collection and catalogue names keep their capitals (Trade Partnership, Everyday Essentials, Trade Catalogue) | Use title case for whole buttons ("Send Trade Enquiry"), or all caps except in eyebrows and product codes |

**[CONFIRM with legal]** where shorter references such as "the stone" are acceptable in running prose (Blueprint 7.1). Until confirmed, use the full term in every formal context.

**Never publish without proof:** minimum order quantities, capacity, lead times, margins, exclusivity, payment or credit terms, delivery commitments, response times, testimonials, retailer logos (Blueprint 7.3).

## 9. Quality and certification

**Eyebrow:** QUALITY & CERTIFICATION
**Heading (H2):** Know exactly what you are selling.

### Card 1: Laboratory-grown diamonds, explained.

A laboratory-grown diamond is diamond grown in a controlled process (HPHT or CVD). It has the same chemical, physical and optical properties as a mined diamond. Origin and quality are separate questions: each stone is graded on its own cut, colour, clarity and carat weight.

**Link:** How laboratory-grown diamonds are made →

Every piece comes with the documents that support what you tell your customer: an IGI certificate for the laboratory-grown diamond and a BIS hallmark with HUID on gold. **[CONFIRM]** the exact documents supplied with each piece and their coverage.

## 14.2 Our Approach page: three pillars

**Design:** Balance, proportion and finish. From a single solitaire to a statement piece, each design is drawn to sell well and wear well.

**Materials:** Laboratory-grown diamonds set in BIS-hallmarked 9K, 14K and 18K gold, platinum and silver. Karat and purity stated on every piece.

**Partnership:** AURNA designs, custom development and white-label supply, so retailers can shape a range that suits their market.

**Claims note:** don't claim superior craftsmanship, ethical sourcing or quality-control standards unless the practice is defined and evidenced.

## 14.3 The Science page: topics

1. What a laboratory-grown diamond is.
2. How laboratory-grown diamonds are made: HPHT and CVD.
3. Origin versus quality: shared material properties, different origins.
4. The 4Cs: cut, colour, clarity and carat weight.
5. IGI certificates: what they show and how to read one.
6. BIS hallmarking and HUID on gold.
7. Quality checks (only real procedures **[CONFIRM]**).
8. Documents supplied with each piece **[CONFIRM]**.

**Editorial rules:** lead with facts; check every technical explanation before publishing; no blanket price, size or value promises; no environmental or sustainability claims; never imply a stone carries a certificate unless that is confirmed for it; never present laboratory-grown diamonds as an inferior alternative.
