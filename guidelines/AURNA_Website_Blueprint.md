# 1. Positioning

## 1.1 What AURNA is

AURNA is a **laboratory-grown diamond and fine jewellery supplier for jewellery retailers**. Its website presents the collections to the standard of a premium consumer brand.

- **Primary audience:** independent jewellers, small chains, boutiques and online jewellery retailers, starting with Tier 2–4 cities in India.
- **Secondary audience:** consumers who find the site. They can browse and ask about a piece. They are not the commercial focus, and AURNA must never undercut its retail partners (Section 8.3).
- **Brand line:** *Brilliance, beautifully considered.*

In short: the site should **look like a premium jewellery house and work like a dependable trade partner.**

## 1.2 Offering

| Offering | Description | Status |
|---|---|---|
| AURNA designs | Selected AURNA designs supplied to retailers | Launch |
| Custom development | Designs developed to a retailer's brief: shape, size, setting, metal | Launch |
| White-label supply | AURNA-made jewellery sold under the retailer's own brand, on agreed terms | Launch |
| Retailer support | Product education material, certificate handling, help explaining laboratory-grown diamonds to customers | Launch: describe only what exists **[CONFIRM]** |
| Loose laboratory-grown diamond supply | Certified loose stones for retailers' own designs | **[DECIDE]** launch or later |
| Replenishment | Re-supply of fast-moving core designs and sizes | **[DECIDE]** launch or later |

**Materials:** laboratory-grown diamonds; 9K, 14K and 18K gold (BIS hallmarked); platinum; silver.
**Fulfilment:** ready stock and made-to-order. Availability is stated per product, only where confirmed.

## 1.3 Brand independence

AURNA is a standalone brand. Its identity, accounts and designs are its own.

- **Own identity:** only AURNA's approved logo, colours, typefaces and imagery on any AURNA surface.
- **Own accounts:** a dedicated domain, email domain, business WhatsApp number, social accounts, analytics and ad accounts.
- **Original designs:** every design shown on the site must be approved by the owner before publishing.
- **Legal identity:** the entity name, GSTIN, address and policy pages shown on the site are confirmed by the owner **[DECIDE]** before the footer and policies are written.

# 2. Website objectives and conversion

## 2.1 Objectives

1. Make jewellery retailers see AURNA as a credible, quality supplier within 10 seconds on a phone.
2. Generate qualified trade enquiries and catalogue requests.
3. Show the product range to a premium standard: product-led, accurate, well photographed.
4. Capture consumer interest without competing with retail partners.
5. Leave room to add a retailer portal, private catalogue and ordering later without rebuilding the site.

## 2.2 Conversion actions (in priority order)

1. **Enquire**: the header CTA. Opens an enquiry form or panel with a selector for trade or consumer enquiries.
2. **Request Trade Catalogue**: the main trade CTA, in the hero, the trade section and the catalogue band.
3. **Chat on WhatsApp**: pre-filled message, product code included where relevant.
4. **Start a Trade Enquiry**: full trade form.
5. **Ask about this design**: product-level enquiry (trade or consumer), with the product code pre-filled.
6. **Explore Collections**: product discovery.

## 2.3 Not included at launch

Shopping cart, checkout, payments, public pricing, retailer login, trade pricing portal, order management, automated custom-design pricing, and a design configurator (the Customise page uses an enquiry form only).

# 3. Visual system

AURNA's look is **white, precise and product-led**. Colour is used sparingly; the jewellery and the typography carry the brand.

> **design.md is the master design document.** This section summarises the visual system; for full specifications (brand archetype, tokens, section rhythm, minimum section heights, components) and in any conflict, design.md applies.

## 3.1 Core rules

| Element | AURNA rule |
|---|---|
| **Dominant surface** | Pure white (#FFFFFF) for most of every page. Very Light Grey (#F1F4F9) for alternate sections, always with a 1 px border. Hero Mist (#E8EDF6) for inner-page heading panels and up to one feature section. Never ivory, cream or beige backgrounds. |
| **Navy** | Used as ink: text, hairlines, icons and primary buttons. Deep Navy backgrounds only for the homepage hero and at most **one** dark call-to-action band per page (pages that end in their own form have none; design.md 5.4). No navy header or other large navy areas. |
| **Header** | White background, navy wordmark and links, 1 px Light Grey (#E5E9F1) bottom border. Sticky. |
| **Footer** | Very Light Grey (#F1F4F9) with navy text; secondary text in Muted Strong (#5A6480). |
| **Hero** | A distinct full-width Deep Navy (#0F1F5C) panel under the white header, with a white upright headline, a gold eyebrow, and either a real product photograph on a white card or one editorial portrait of AURNA pieces being worn (design.md 6.4, 7.2). No gradients, glows, textures or decorative motifs. |
| **Sections** | Each section reads as its own block: backgrounds alternate with 1 px borders, and every section has a minimum height and generous padding (design.md 5.4–5.5). |
| **Gold** | In the logo artwork, plus a small non-text marker (e.g., a 2 px bar beside section eyebrows or under the active navigation item). Never used for text on white (it fails contrast), never as dividing rules, never as filled areas. |
| **Links and hover** | Sapphire #13348C. |
| **Typography** | Cormorant Garamond for display headings; DM Sans for body text, navigation and UI. |
| **Buttons** | Square corners (2 px radius maximum). Primary: filled Deep Navy #0F1F5C with white text, one per view in page content (the header Enquire button is exempt). Secondary: 1 px navy outline. |
| **Signature device** | Typography and data: product codes (e.g., AR-SOL-01) and specification lines set as a consistent, recognisable typographic system. No symbol or pattern motif. |
| **Imagery** | Product first: 45° hero angle, top and side views, true colour, scale-reference shots. Light editorial use only (e.g., bench or quality-check detail). One exception: the homepage hero may show AURNA pieces worn by a single model, with the jewellery clearly visible. No couples, proposals or lifestyle scenes elsewhere. |
| **Voice** | Precise, professional, partner-focused (Section 4). |

## 3.3 Typography

- **Display:** Cormorant Garamond for H1–H3 and product names.
- **Body and UI:** DM Sans for body text, navigation, buttons, forms, product codes and specifications.
- **Logo:** always the approved AURNA wordmark artwork (SVG/PNG), never retyped in a font.
- **Performance:** limit font weights and files for mobile networks.

## 3.4 Layout and imagery

- Generous white space, consistent content widths, clean responsive grids.
- Real product photography only. No AI-generated or illustrated product imagery where it could misrepresent a design.
- Fixed photo order per product: 45° hero view, then top view, then side view.
- Subtle transitions only; no sliders or carousels that hide content.
- Mobile-first: most retailers will read on a phone.
- WCAG AA contrast on all text; real form labels; descriptive alt text.

# 7. Compliance

These rules apply to all website copy, the catalogue and product labels.

## 7.1 Laboratory-grown diamond naming (BIS IS 19469:2025)

- Use **"laboratory-grown diamond"** (or "laboratory-created diamond") in product specs, labels, certificates, product cards, the proof strip, forms and SEO titles.
- Never use the bare word "diamond" in a way that implies natural origin. Form fields read "Stone shape" or "Laboratory-grown diamond shape", not "Diamond shape".
- Never use "LGD", "lab diamond", "lab-grown" (abbreviated), "cultured", "synthetic" or "artificial" in formal copy or labels.
- Disclose production method (CVD/HPHT) and any treatments where required.
- **[CONFIRM with legal]** where shorter references (e.g., "the stone") are acceptable in running prose and in SEO keywords.

## 7.2 Metals

- State karat and BIS hallmark (HUID) plainly for gold.
- State platinum and silver purity only as documented **[CONFIRM]**.

## 7.3 Claims register

Before anything is published, list every claim with its proof: certification coverage, hallmarking, lead times, minimum order quantities, capacity, support services, warranty.

**Never published without proof:** minimum order quantities, production capacity, lead times, margins, exclusivity, payment or credit terms, delivery commitments, testimonials, retailer logos, sustainability or ethics claims.

# 8. Enquiries and lead management

## 8.1 Channels

- **WhatsApp Business:** a dedicated AURNA number, with pre-filled messages by context (e.g., "Hi AURNA, I'd like details on AR-SOL-01").
- **Forms:** submit to a monitored business inbox or CRM; show a confirmation message; include spam protection, consent and a privacy notice; never put personal data in URLs or page code. A copy-to-clipboard interaction is not a substitute for real submission.
- **Response time:** publish only a target the team can keep **[DECIDE]**.

## 8.2 Trade forms

**Request Trade Catalogue (short):** business/store name · contact name · city · phone/WhatsApp · business email · business type (independent jeweller, chain, boutique, online retailer, other) · consent.

**Trade enquiry (full):** the short fields plus GSTIN (optional) · interest (AURNA designs / custom development / white-label / loose stones **[if offered]** / other) · categories of interest · approximate volume (optional ranges) · requirements · consent.

**Catalogue delivery:** manual at launch, after reviewing each enquiry: sent as a PDF or over WhatsApp from the AURNA Catalogue (Edition 01). Send only the updated Edition 01 (Section 3.6: white background, compliant wording). The current ivory version uses "lab-grown" and must not be sent.

## 8.3 Consumer enquiries (showroom model)

- Consumers browse freely; product cards show "Ask about this design".
- **Short form:** name · city · phone/WhatsApp · email · design code · message · consent.
- **Routing:** once partner retailers are in place, consumer enquiries go to a partner jeweller in or near the consumer's city.
- **Until then:** AURNA may respond directly. Prices are quoted on request only, with an internal floor at or above the price a partner retailer would charge, so AURNA never undercuts a partner. **[DECIDE]** the exact floor rule.
- Consumer custom requests go through the consumer route on the Customise page (enquiry form only; no configurator or automated pricing). **[DECIDE]** how much prominence the consumer route gets on that page.
