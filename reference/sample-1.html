FOR RETAILERS & JEWELLERY BUSINESSES

A stronger jewellery offering. A more flexible way to grow.

The jewellery retail landscape is changing. As organised retail expands into more markets, independent retailers have an opportunity to stand out through distinctive collections, personal customer relationships and a more careful approach to inventory.

AURNA works with jewellery retailers and businesses through curated collections, custom development and flexible supply partnerships.

A more inventory-conscious approach

Offer a wider range without holding it all in stock.

Precious-metal inventory can tie up significant working capital, particularly when metal prices are high. AURNA aims to help retail partners take a more inventory-conscious approach through selected designs, made-to-order options and customised supply arrangements.

The aim is to give retailers more flexibility in how they present and fulfil their jewellery offering.

Stay distinctive in a changing market

Compete through individuality, not just scale.

As organised jewellery brands expand into more cities, independent retailers need clear reasons for customers to choose them. Distinctive designs, personalised options and the ability to respond to individual customer requests help create that difference.

UNDERSTANDING LABORATORY-GROWN DIAMONDS

The science behind the brilliance.

Laboratory-grown diamonds are crystallised carbon, with the same chemical, physical and optical properties as mined diamonds. Only the origin differs: they are grown using HPHT or CVD processes.
