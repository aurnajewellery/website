Privacy notice

How we collect, use and protect the personal data you share with us.

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Draft for legal review. This policy has not yet been approved. It is shown in preview only.

Who we are

This website is run by AURNA Jewellery ("AURNA", "we", "us"). We supply laboratory-grown diamond jewellery to jewellery retailers and businesses, and respond to enquiries from individual buyers. We are responsible for the personal data described in this notice.

Our registered address is .

What we collect

We collect only what you choose to give us:

  • Enquiry and catalogue forms: your name, business or store name, role, city, state or country, phone or WhatsApp number, email address, GSTIN (if you give it), business type, your interests and requirements, design codes and any message you write.
  • Custom design requests: the details of the piece you describe and, where the upload option is available, any reference images you attach.
  • WhatsApp: if you contact us on WhatsApp, your WhatsApp name, number and the messages you send.
  • Technical data: the page you sent a form from and the time it was sent.

We don't ask for payment details, government identification numbers or passwords on this website.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss your requirements.
  • To review catalogue requests and send the trade catalogue.
  • To prepare specifications, quotations and orders that you ask for.
  • To keep a record of our conversations with you.

We use your data on the basis of the consent you give when you tick the box on our forms, or when you message us. We don't sell your personal data, and we don't use it for advertising profiles.

Who we share it with

We share personal data only as needed to run our business:

  • Service providers who host this website, deliver form submissions to our inbox, and provide email and WhatsApp messaging. They process data on our instructions.
  • Partner jewellers: if you are an individual buyer and ask about a design, we may suggest a partner jeweller near you. We will share your details with them only if you agree.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

Some providers may store data outside India. Where they do, we use providers that protect it to a standard consistent with Indian law.

Fonts and links to other services

This website uses fonts loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address with Google when a page loads. Links to WhatsApp and Instagram take you to services run by Meta, whose own privacy policies apply. This website doesn't use advertising or analytics cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry data for as long as we need it to respond to you and to manage any business relationship that follows, and then for any period the law requires us to keep records. We then delete it.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it
  • correct, complete or update it
  • delete it, where we no longer need it
  • stop using it, by withdrawing your consent at any time

You can also nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf, and raise a grievance with us. If you aren't satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

The website is served over an encrypted connection, and we limit access to enquiry data to the people who need it to respond to you. No method of sending or storing data is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect it.

Children

Our website and services are intended for adults and businesses. We don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact and grievances

To use your rights or raise a concern, contact us through our contact page. We'll respond within the time the law allows.
