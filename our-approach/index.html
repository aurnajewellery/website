Our Approach

A jewellery supplier that works around your store.

AURNA makes laboratory-grown diamond jewellery in gold, platinum and silver for jewellery retailers. We work as a supply partner: our designs, designs developed to your brief, or our pieces under your own brand.

How we work

Design, materials and partnership.

  • Design

    Balance, proportion and finish. From a single solitaire to a statement piece, each design is drawn to sell well and wear well.

  • Materials

    Laboratory-grown diamonds set in BIS-hallmarked 9K, 14K and 18K gold, platinum and silver. Karat and purity are stated on every piece.

  • Partnership

    AURNA designs, custom development and white-label supply, so retailers can shape a range that suits their market.

Who we work with

Built for jewellery retailers.

Independent jewellers, small chains, boutiques and online jewellery retailers in India.

Our job is to give your store a dependable supply of well-made laboratory-grown diamond jewellery, with the specifications and documents your team needs to sell it with confidence. Consumers can browse the collection and ask about a piece, but AURNA's focus is its retail partners.

Three ways to work with AURNA

  1. AURNA designsChoose from the current collection for your store.
  2. Custom developmentDesigns developed to your brief: shape, size, setting and metal.
  3. White-label supplyAURNA-made pieces under your own brand, on agreed terms.

Materials and standards

Specified plainly, documented properly.

Every AURNA piece is described by code, stone, carat weight and metal, using the wording Indian standards require.

Laboratory-grown diamonds

Described as "laboratory-grown diamond" on every label and document, as IS 19469:2025 requires. Graded on the 4Cs, with IGI certificates.

IGI certificates explained

Gold, platinum and silver

Gold in 9K, 14K and 18K with a BIS hallmark and HUID. Platinum and silver purity stated as documented.
